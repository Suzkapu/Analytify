The complete game

How Song League works

Bring three to five friends, recommend discoveries, and let everyone’s real Spotify rankings decide the winner.

1

Create your group

Invite normal Analytify accounts. Everyone needs Cloud Backup so the game has trusted daily Top Songs snapshots.

2

Choose one Friday discovery

Your song must be absent from a strict majority of opponents and cannot already be in anyone else’s Top 20.

3

Create your Spotify playlist

Use the button below the recommendations to create your private Weekly Picks playlist. Accepted picks update it automatically, and Friday replaces the previous week.

4

Climb the standings

A recommendation scores from every opponent’s daily Top Songs for four weeks. Those points remain in the permanent standings.

Scoring formula List size − rank + 1

Your own ranking never awards points to your song.
