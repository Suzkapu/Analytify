Simon Praher, at the address and email shown in the Legal Notice above, is the controller for Analytify. No data protection officer has been appointed because Analytify does not carry out processing that requires one. This notice applies before you connect Spotify and whenever you use Analytify.

No ads or tracking. Analytify does not use advertising, analytics, fingerprinting, social plugins, or cross-site tracking. A fresh logged-out visit creates no account, feature, stats, or tracking record.

What is processed and why

Each card describes one processing activity. “Contract” means GDPR Article 6(1)(b); “legitimate interests” means Article 6(1)(f).

Open the website and keep it secure

Data and source
IP address, request time, requested path, response status, browser/device information, and security errors, sent by your device.
Purpose and legal basis
Deliver and update the website, diagnose failures, and prevent abuse. Contract; and legitimate interests in reliable, secure operation.
Recipients and transfers
The Oracle-hosted web server and operator. Your browser obtains Spotify artwork directly from Spotify's content delivery network. Those providers may process data outside the EEA under their published transfer safeguards.
Retention
Application caches remain until the browser or service worker clears them. Server security and access logs are kept only for the operational period needed to diagnose incidents and are then deleted or rotated.
Required?
Basic request data is required to deliver the site. No advertising or analytics identifier is required or created.

Connect Spotify and show your library, stats, and history

Data and source
Spotify user ID, display name, profile image, access/refresh tokens, playlists and saved tracks, recently played items, top songs, artists and genres, and related Spotify catalog metadata. Spotify supplies this after you authorize Analytify. For a personal Spotify app, you also provide its public Client ID; Analytify never requests its Client Secret.
Purpose and legal basis
Authenticate your Spotify connection and provide the pages and comparisons you request. Contract.
Recipients and transfers
Spotify's API/CDN and your browser. Hosted authentication and token operations also use Supabase and the Oracle worker. Spotify and Supabase may use locations outside the EEA with their stated safeguards.
Retention
Local records remain until you clear local data or sign out. Access tokens expire according to Spotify; refresh credentials remain until revoked, disconnected, or deleted. Local history remains until you delete it.
Required?
A Spotify connection and the requested Spotify data are required for these features. Cloud Backup is not required.

Cloud identity, backup, and scheduled updates

Data and source
Verified Spotify ID, display name and image; a Supabase account ID; selected snapshots, history and feature caches; schedule settings and run status; and, only for unattended updates, an encrypted Spotify refresh token. The data comes from you, your browser, Spotify, and scheduled task results.
Purpose and legal basis
Provide the cloud, recovery, and scheduled features you explicitly enable. Contract. Security audit records are also processed under legitimate interests.
Recipients and transfers
Supabase hosts authentication/database functions; the Oracle worker performs scheduled jobs; Spotify receives refresh and data requests. International transfers depend on those providers' contractual safeguards.
Retention
Account-linked data remains until you delete Cloud Backup or the account. The unattended credential can be removed separately. Successful/cancelled task logs are kept 30 days; failed logs are redacted after 30 days and deleted after 90 days.
Required?
Optional. A personal-app cloud identity is anonymous and browser-bound: no email, phone, password, or recovery identity is requested.

Playlist sharing, stats access, and Compare Rooms

Data and source
Your profile, selected playlist metadata and tracks, selected stats/snapshots, invitations, access decisions, room membership, and public-playlist links. Data comes from you, Spotify, and the people who invite or share with you.
Purpose and legal basis
Share only with the people you select and run requested comparisons. Contract.
Recipients and transfers
The selected participants, Supabase, Oracle, and Spotify when a playlist must be read. Do not share a link with someone who should not receive it.
Retention
Claimed shares remain until revoked or removed; unclaimed share links expire after 7 days and revoked share tombstones after 30 days. Compare Rooms expire after 12 hours; invitations after 30 minutes; expired/revoked room records are deleted within 30 days.
Required?
Optional. The selected content and participant identity are required only for that sharing or comparison.

Song League

Data and source
Member profile, league role, invitations, recommendations, Spotify song/catalog details, rankings, points, playlist state, and activity times. This comes from members, Spotify, and the ranking calculation.
Purpose and legal basis
Run the league, calculate standings, and maintain requested league playlists. Contract.
Recipients and transfers
Other members of that league, Supabase, Oracle, and Spotify for song and playlist operations.
Retention
League history remains until the owner deletes the league or account deletion removes the member link. Recommendations can remain in the generated playlist for their four-week active window. Invite links normally expire after 7 days.
Required?
Optional. Profile, recommendation, and scoring data are required only while participating.

No. Withdrawing consent stops future notifications and does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Record the agreement, complete requested flows, preserve local features, and update the app safely. Contract; legal obligation where proof of terms is required; and legitimate interests in reliable operation.

Where the data comes from

You provide data directly through your choices and messages. Spotify provides account, listening, playlist, and catalog data after authorization. Other Analytify users provide data when they invite you, share with you, include you in a league, request access, or file a report. Analytify also creates technical status, ranking, and security records while providing those features.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and a copy: ask what personal data is processed and receive a copy.
  • Correction: correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data.
  • Deletion: delete local data, Cloud Backup, sharing access, scheduled credentials, or your account from Data & account, or ask the operator.
  • Restriction: ask that processing be limited while a dispute is checked.
  • Portability: receive data you provided in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format where Article 20 applies.
  • Object: object to processing based on legitimate interests. Analytify will stop unless compelling grounds or legal claims require it.
  • Withdraw consent: disable notification categories at any time. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing.

Send a rights request to analytify.taking893@aleeas.com. Enough information may be requested to verify that the data belongs to you. Analytify will respond without undue delay and normally within one month; a complex request may be extended by up to two further months with an explanation. You may also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at.

Automated results, not automated decisions

Analytify automatically calculates listening trends, playlist comparisons, Song League rankings, and scores from the selected Spotify data and league rules. These are entertainment/information results. They do not produce legal or similarly significant effects, and Analytify does not use solely automated decision-making of the kind described in GDPR Article 22.

International transfers and provider details

Spotify, Supabase, Oracle, and a device's Web Push provider are separate providers involved only where described above. Some may process data outside the EEA. Analytify relies on the provider's applicable adequacy route or contractual safeguards, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses where used. Provider locations and safeguards can change; see Spotify's Privacy Policy and Supabase's Privacy Policy, or ask the operator for the current safeguard relevant to your data.

Deleting data and changing this notice

Use Data & account to clear local data, delete cloud data, remove unattended Spotify access, and manage notifications. Revoking Spotify access stops future API access but does not itself erase data already stored by Analytify. Shared catalog facts such as artist and song names may remain after account deletion, but the link to your identity is removed.

Material changes receive a new version and require acknowledgement before a new Spotify connection or authorization. Smaller clarifications are published here with a new effective date. The previous legal version remains identifiable in the repository history.
