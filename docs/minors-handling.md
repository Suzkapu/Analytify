# Eligibility and minors-handling procedure

Last reviewed: 23 September 2026

Analytify is available only to people aged 14 or older. A user under 18 must have the agreement of a parent or legal guardian and must also be eligible to use their Spotify account under Spotify's terms. Analytify does not request, infer, or store dates of birth.

This 14-year minimum follows [section 4(4) of the Austrian Data Protection Act](https://ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Anlage=&Artikel=2&Gesetzesnummer=10001597&Paragraf=4&Uebergangsrecht=), which sets 14 as the threshold for a child's own consent to information-society processing. It does not replace [Spotify's separate Austrian eligibility rules](https://www.spotify.com/at/legal/end-user-agreement/plain/) or a parent or guardian's agreement where those rules require it.

## Report and review

Anyone can report suspected ineligible use to `analytify.taking893@aleeas.com` or through the in-app report control. The operator records the report in the moderation inbox and starts review promptly. The report must describe the concern without including identity documents or a date of birth.

The operator tells the affected user that access is under review and provides a way to explain that the report is mistaken. The operator must not request a passport, identity card, date of birth, or other age-verification document. If the report cannot be substantiated, it is closed without action and the user is notified.

## Confirmed ineligibility

When the operator confirms that the user is below the applicable minimum or otherwise not eligible:

1. Refuse further use and prevent the cloud identity from participating in sharing, Compare Rooms, notifications, or Song Leagues.
2. Delete the stored Spotify credential first, which stops scheduled access. Tell the user or guardian how to remove Analytify from the Spotify account's connected-apps page so Spotify also revokes the grant.
3. Delete the Analytify cloud profile through the existing **Delete cloud data and leave shared features** account-deletion flow. This removes linked snapshots, history, schedules, notification devices, shares, stats grants, and league membership under the database's deletion rules.
4. Tell the user or guardian to clear Analytify's local data on every device, because browser-only data cannot be erased remotely.
5. Record only the moderation decision, actions, and timestamps needed to show that the request was handled. Do not retain evidence that contains unnecessary child data.

If immediate deletion would prevent investigation of a safety incident or compliance with a binding legal obligation, the operator restricts access, isolates the minimum necessary record, documents the reason and review date, and deletes it as soon as that reason ends.

## Mistakes and contact

A user or guardian who believes a block or deletion decision was mistaken can reply to the decision notice or email the operator. The operator reviews the challenge separately and does not ask for a date of birth. Restoring service requires confirmation that both Analytify's and Spotify's eligibility rules are met.

This procedure is operational guidance, not a request for children to submit personal information.
